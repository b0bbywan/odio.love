odio.love is a static informational website. It has **no user accounts, no forms, no newsletter, and no advertising**. We collect as little data as technically possible, and none of it is used to build a profile of you.

## Cookies

This site sets **no cookies**, neither first-party nor third-party.

## Analytics

We use **Vercel Web Analytics** to understand aggregate traffic (page views, referrers, country-level geolocation, device and browser type). It is **cookieless**: visitors are identified only by a temporary hash derived from the incoming request, which is automatically discarded after 24 hours. No IP address, no persistent identifier, and no cross-site tracking are stored. The data cannot be used to identify or re-identify you.

For details, see Vercel's [Web Analytics privacy documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy).

## Server logs

As with any website, our host (Vercel) may process technical request data (including IP addresses) transiently, for the purposes of serving content, security, and abuse prevention. This processing rests on our legitimate interest in operating a secure service, in accordance with [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## External links

This site links to third-party services (GitHub, Discord, Vercel, and the projects odio builds on). Once you follow such a link, the destination's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for their practices.

## Your rights

Because the analytics data is anonymous and no personal identifiers are stored, we hold no personal data that could be linked back to you. Any question about privacy on this site can be sent to <Email user="contact" domain="odio.love" />. If you believe your data protection rights are affected, you may also contact your local supervisory authority.
